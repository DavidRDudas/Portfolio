Superposition

A single eigenstate is stationary — |ψ|² never changes. Mix two and the cross term beats at (E₂−E₁)/ℏ: the cloud sloshes, and that oscillating dipole is what radiates a photon.

Reading it

  • Every dot is one draw from |ψ|² — where the electron would be found.
  • The grains drift along the real probability current, vφ = ℏm/(μr sinθ). A stationary state is stationary, not static: the density never changes while the electron keeps circulating.
  • Hue (in glow style) is the phase of ψ, winding once per unit of m.
  • Dark shells are nodes, where ψ passes through zero: n−ℓ−1 radial, ℓ angular.
  • Scale grows as n² — 8g is ~40× wider than 1s.

Hydrogen only. One electron means the Schrödinger equation is exactly solvable, so nothing here is fitted or approximated. Helium onward has no closed form — two electrons repelling each other needs Hartree–Fock or DFT, and “orbital” stops being an exact object.
